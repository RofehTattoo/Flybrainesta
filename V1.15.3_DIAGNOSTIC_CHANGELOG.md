# FlyBrain V1.15.3-DIAGNOSTIC

## Purpose
Read-only instrumentation phase for causal diagnosis. No FBC103, FBD104, FBR-10 connectivity, or behavioral stimulus-to-command rules are changed.

## Added telemetry
- Per-neuron `lastSynDrive` captured immediately before LIF thresholding.
- Motor drive: signed mean, mean absolute magnitude, and peak across VNC motor neurons.
- Motor-drive summaries by LEG/WING/NECK/ABDOMEN/HALTERE/OTHER.
- Left/right leg motor drive.
- Physical acceleration.
- Wall distance and `wallSignal`.
- Diagnostic card readout for motor drive, body kinematics and wall state.

## Scientific constraint
Telemetry is read-only and never feeds back into neural dynamics or body mechanics.
