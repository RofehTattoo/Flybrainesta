# FlyBrain V1.19.32 — Regression Recovery Audit

This release is intentionally based on the last mechanically stable V1.19.30 CI-FIX1 baseline.

## Why V1.19.31 was rejected

The V1.19.31 ground-reaction refactor introduced two locomotion regressions:

1. `swingFraction` was inverted (`if (s) smooth else 0`) so swing kinematics were evaluated during stance.
2. The active neural-motor stance propulsion was replaced by passive slip-friction against the body/foot velocity. That changes propulsion into a self-braking contact model and sharply reduces sustained forward speed.

The CI FIX2 normalization of `forwardForceProxy` only changed a diagnostic scale. It did not restore the lost propulsion or the behavioral dynamics, so a green CI signal would not constitute behavioral recovery.

## Recovery policy

V1.19.32 restores the complete V1.19.30 locomotion/body mechanics unchanged. It does not attempt another speculative wall-escape controller. Wall behavior should now be debugged from this stable baseline using telemetry of mechanosensory L/R -> DN L/R -> VNC/MN L/R -> six-leg loads -> contact/yaw.

## FBR-10 scope

No FBR-10 selection, ORN census, connectome edges, sensory topology, or food pathway is changed by this recovery.
