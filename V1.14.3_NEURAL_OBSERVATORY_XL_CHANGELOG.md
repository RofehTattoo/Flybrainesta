# FlyBrain V1.14.3 — Neural Observatory XL

## Goal
Make the read-only neural diagnostics legible at a glance on a phone and in screen recordings, while making the lower connectome visualization cleaner in dense regions.

## Presentation-only changes
- Diagnostic card in the white simulation area is now sized using dp/sp rather than raw pixels.
- Card uses the available white area at nearly full width and is substantially taller.
- Five top DNs and five top motor neurons remain visible with real MaleCNS body IDs, functional roles, activity percentages and compact bars.
- Added explicit stimulus state (`NINGUNO`, `COMIDA`, `LUZ`, `PELIGRO`) to the diagnostic header.
- Added compact high-value metrics: spikes/s, S/C/DN/M population rates, sensory input drive, movement and operational state.
- Kept the rolling 5 s Neural/DN/Motor history strip.
- Lower CNS map edges are substantially thinner and lower-alpha. Activity is encoded primarily through brightness/alpha instead of large stroke diameter.
- Dense blue and gray bundles therefore obscure fewer neighboring neurons/regions while preserving the represented real edges.

## Not changed
No connectome record, edge weight, LIF equation, sensory current, neurotransmitter mapping, or body-control rule was changed by this release. The changes are observability/presentation only.
