# FlyBrain V1.19.21 — anatomical visual placement correction

## Objective

Correct the 2D neural observatory so that visible activation is placed in an
anatomically meaningful location for the *Drosophila melanogaster* nervous
system, without modifying FBR-10 topology, neural dynamics, sensory injection,
or body mechanics.

## Source-grounded policy

- Primary visual receptor neurons are shown at the compound-eye side from the
  official side annotation; visual relay neurons remain in the optic lobes.
- Primary olfactory receptor neurons are shown at the antenna/maxillary sensory
  origin, while olfactory central relay families may be shown in the antennal
  lobe.
- Gustatory neurons are split by the existing official runtime `gustSite`:
  tarsal receptors are placed at the six-foot/peripheral entry region;
  labellar/pharyngeal receptors are placed in the SEZ.
- Antennal mechanosensory neurons associated with Johnston's organ are placed
  in the AMMC; leg/chordotonal mechanosensory populations are placed in the VNC
  entry region.
- VNC motor neurons are placed in the VNC and leg groups preserve the six
  anatomical LF/LM/LH/RF/RM/RH groupings already decoded from the official
  `vnc_motor_semantics.tsv` layer.
- Ascending neurons are shown along the VNC/cervical path; descending neurons
  are shown in the lower brain-to-VNC region.
- Central neurons are assigned only to conservative cell-type families when
  the official type vocabulary supports it (mushroom body, central complex,
  antennal lobe, SEZ); otherwise they remain in `CENTRAL_BRAIN` rather than
  receiving a false exact neuropil coordinate.

## Presentation contract

The 2D map is a schematic projection, not a 3D anatomical reconstruction.
Actual retained connectome edges are the only synaptic links drawn. An active
retained neuron outside the 320-node representative graph is still drawn as a
small active marker at its anatomical reference position, so firing is not
hidden merely because the representative graph sample did not include it.

This map is presentation-only and never feeds values back into the simulator.
