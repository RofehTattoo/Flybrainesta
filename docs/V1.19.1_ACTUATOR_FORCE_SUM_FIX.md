# V1.19.1 — Actuator force-summation correction

## Failure

GitHub Actions compiled Kotlin successfully, then failed
`LeggedSensorimotorActuatorTest.bilateralMotorOutput_drivesSixLegMechanicsAndFeedback`.
The bilateral gait generated nonzero stance-foot propulsion, but the actuator divided
the sum of all six foot contributions by six. This treated a net body force as a
per-leg mean and suppressed forward velocity below the unit-test expectation.

## Correction

`LeggedSensorimotorActuator` now sums the bounded propulsive contributions from
stance feet and saturates the combined normalized force at `0.66`. The source of
propulsion remains backward foot motion during stance; no direct motor-rate-to-speed
shortcut, food steering, synthetic turning, or connectome edit was introduced.

## Scope

Only the mechanical conversion from per-leg stance force to body acceleration
changed. FBR-10/FBC103, neural dynamics, sensory inputs, yaw calculation, wall
constraint, feeding logic, and version identity remain unchanged.
