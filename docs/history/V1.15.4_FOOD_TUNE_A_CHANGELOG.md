# FlyBrain V1.15.5-FOOD-TUNE-B

Single-parameter food stimulus experiment derived from V1.15.3-DIAGNOSTIC.

## Change
- Olfactory source gain in `sense()` increased from `2.25f` to `3.00f`, represented by `FOOD_OLF_GAIN`.
- Olfactory distance kernel remains unchanged (`gaussian(d, .58f)`).
- `SENSORY_OLF_GAIN` remains `1.00f`.

## Explicitly unchanged
- FBC103 binary and FBR-10 connectivity.
- FBD104 dynamics layer.
- VNC motor semantics.
- Body mechanics / `driveBody()`.
- Light, danger, taste and mechanosensory gains.
- No direct food-to-turn or food-to-movement command added.

## Experiment
FOOD-TUNE-A: test whether stronger olfactory input produces earlier/stronger neural propagation and, without changing motor translation, reveals food-directed motor asymmetry.

## Version
Android `1.15.5`, versionCode `123`.
