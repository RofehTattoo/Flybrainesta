# FlyBrain V1.19.7 — neural feeding dwell and embodied braking

## Why
V1.19.6 gated gait activity, but the body retained forward velocity through the
actuator's ordinary damping. In addition, ingestion required gustatory spikes in
the exact same frame as ingestion-motor spikes, despite neural propagation delay.

## Changes
- Neural taste/proboscis/ingestion events renew a finite dwell (0.65 / 1.80 / 2.80 s).
- Feeding context is temporal: ingestion motor output is associated with recent
  pharyngeal contact (0.35 s), recent taste and proboscis neural context.
- The actuator translates measured walk-off activation into physical deceleration
  as well as gait suppression, so momentum does not carry the fly straight over food.
- Food coordinates and `foodOn` remain absent from the leg actuator.
- FBR-10 neuron and edge data are unchanged.

These are simulation parameters and structural corrections; biological realism
still needs validation against the in-app behavior/video.
