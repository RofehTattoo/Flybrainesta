# FlyBrain V1.19.1 — Sensorimotor Closed Loop Correction

Corrects observed V1.19.0 failures: tonic LEG activity being interpreted as continuous propulsion, wall contact not feeding back into the actuator, dead pause telemetry, and feeding being limited to tarsal taste.

The FBR-10/FBC103 graph remains immutable. No synthetic neuron/edge or food-to-heading/speed/eat shortcut is introduced.

## Locomotion
Six official LEG groups are maintained. Per-leg tonic baseline is estimated from the measured firing rate; phasic excess/rise above that baseline drives gait activation. Propulsion comes from backward stance-foot motion. Yaw comes from left/right stance reaction imbalance rather than a direct steering command. The actuator therefore decelerates naturally when phasic motor output ceases.

## Pauses
Pause counters are now updated from measured physical speed with hysteresis and are explicitly excluded during wall contact. V1.19.1 does not schedule or inject random pauses. A pause is counted only when the embodied system actually stops; whether spontaneous pause bouts occur depends on whether the retained neural circuit produces corresponding stop/low-walk episodes.

## Walls
The body uses a conservative physical/render envelope. Predicted wall contact removes only the outward world-velocity component and is fed back to the actuator as wall pressure/proprioceptive evidence. No heading reflection or bounce is used.

## Feeding
The sensory map derives official gustatory site classes: TARSAL, LABELLAR, PHARYNGEAL, OTHER. Runtime samples the six actual foot tips plus anterior labellar and pharyngeal locations. Food amount is depleted only after a measured neural ingestion episode requiring gustatory context, pharyngeal contact, proboscis context, and retained ingestion motor spikes.
