# FlyBrain V1.19.2 — Audio + wing anatomy refinement (PATCH 2)

## Why the previous wing drawing was rejected
The previous patch accidentally drew two wing silhouettes per side (a "far" and
"near" pair). Drosophila melanogaster has one forewing per side. This patch removes
the extra pair entirely.

## Wing representation
- Two wings only: left and right.
- Lateral attachment to the thorax in the fly's local body frame.
- Broad, rounded distal margin and narrow proximal articulation.
- Costa, four principal longitudinal vein traces and two short cross-veins.
- Small neural-state-driven articulation, with no independent animation clock.
- No 3D rendering and no photorealistic asset.

The geometry is intentionally a clean anatomical approximation based on the user's
dorsal reference image; it is not a taxonomic plate or a pixel-faithful micrograph.

## Buzz
- Rebuilt as a 4-second loop around 192 Hz rather than a simple single-frequency tone.
- Includes higher harmonics, slow wingbeat-frequency drift, amplitude modulation and
  low-level aerodynamic texture.
- Playback pitch variation reduced to 0.97–1.035x to avoid obvious electronic
  "pitch bending".
- Sound remains tied to actual wing/body activity.

Scientific basis:
Male D. melanogaster sustained-flight wingbeat frequencies have been reported
roughly 170–220 Hz, with a median around 191.7 Hz in one study; another source reports
an average around 200 Hz and notes that frequency varies with conditions. Flight sounds
are complex and not well represented by a single pure harmonic.
