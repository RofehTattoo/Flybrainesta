# FlyBrain V1.19.3 — locomotion speed + antenna + buzz refinement

## Terrestrial locomotion
The leg actuator is now calibrated toward the measured high-speed walking regime
of adult Drosophila rather than the previous visibly sluggish regime:
- max leg phase: 16 Hz (close to the ~60 ms step period reported at fast walking);
- larger foot stroke;
- higher mechanical force-to-body-speed gain;
- normalized forward-speed ceiling increased to 5.0 arena-units/s.

The renderer's fly body is ~0.20 normalized arena units long, so the new ceiling is
about 25 body-lengths/s. Published measurements include typical straight-walking
speeds around 28 mm/s and instantaneous speeds above 50 mm/s; with ~2.04 mm flies
this corresponds to roughly 14–25 body-lengths/s. The target is therefore a fast
but biologically grounded regime, not an arbitrary multiplier.

## Antennae
The previous long stalks were replaced by compact two-segment antennae with short
aristae. The new projection is roughly half the previous length and stays close to
the head.

## Abdomen / shadow
Removed the rotated ground-shadow ellipse that visually appeared as a gray vertical
stripe near the male abdominal tip.

## Audio
Added weak harmonics 11–16 and mild nonlinear spectral shaping while retaining the
194 Hz-centered flight buzz and low-level air texture. The result is intentionally
an approximation, not a laboratory recording.

## Connectome
FBR-10/FBR-10-OLF2-MOTORROUTE neuron and edge data are not modified.
