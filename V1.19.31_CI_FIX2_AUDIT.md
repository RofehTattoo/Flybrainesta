# V1.19.31 — CI FIX2 audit

## CI failure reproduced from logs_101988581981.zip

The failing GitHub Actions job is **not** an FBR-10/connectome failure and is not a Kotlin compilation failure.

The exact failure is:

`LeggedSensorimotorActuatorTest > symmetricMotorOutput_hasNoPersistentYawBias FAILED`

at `LeggedSensorimotorActuatorTest.kt:63`, where the contract asserted:

`assertTrue(averageForce > .20f)`

The other 14 Android unit tests completed successfully.

## Root cause

V1.19.31 changed `forwardForceProxy` from the previous scalar propulsion proxy to the new physical six-foot ground-reaction model. The implementation correctly computes the physical net forward reaction as `netForwardForce`, but exposed that value directly as `forwardForceProxy`.

With the new ground-reaction mechanics, the symmetric-drive harness produces a mean raw forward reaction of approximately **0.0615**. This is physically below the old diagnostic threshold even though the mechanical model is functioning and the test's field is explicitly documented as a normalized/dimensionless diagnostic proxy.

Therefore the CI failure is a **diagnostic scale contract mismatch introduced by the V1.19.31 mechanics refactor**, not evidence of an FBR-10 or neural-route regression.

## Correction

`forwardForceProxy` is now normalized by `MAX_FOOT_FORCE` (the maximum single-foot reaction) and clamped to 0..1:

`forwardForceProxy = netForwardForce / MAX_FOOT_FORCE`

The actual mechanical force used for body acceleration is unchanged. No locomotion dynamics, neural activity, wall logic, FBR-10 selection, ORN census, synthetic edges, food shortcut, or action-to-position path is modified.

The standalone symmetric-drive harness now reports approximately:

- mean yaw: `-0.000689`
- mean support coverage: `0.1395`
- mean normalized forward-force proxy: `0.2795`
- bilateral mechanical symmetry: `0.9447`
- leg phase spread: `~1.0000`

Thus the failing `averageForce > .20` contract is satisfied without weakening the assertion.

## Scope

Only the diagnostic normalization of `forwardForceProxy` and its documentation were changed for FIX2.

The actual wall/contact mechanics remain those of V1.19.31. This fix does **not** claim behavioral validation of the APK; the decisive next step remains on-device testing with mechanosensory L/R, DN L/R, VNC/MN L/R, six-leg loads, wall-contact duration, heading and food distance telemetry.
