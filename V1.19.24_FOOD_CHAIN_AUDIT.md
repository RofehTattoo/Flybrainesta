Focus: causal validation of FOOD → bilateral olfaction/gustation → retained MaleCNS network → VNC/feeding outputs → six-leg body mechanics.

## Biological limitation
The odor field remains a simplified Gaussian environmental field and does not model turbulent plume transport or wind. This release does not add such a model because doing so without a validated environmental dataset would be parameter invention.

## Additional cleanup
- Removed the unused `feedingPause*` software state from the runtime. It never drove the actuator in V1.19.23, but retaining it was misleading and created a latent risk of reintroducing a food→body shortcut.
- Feeding locomotor arrest is now represented only by measured retained walk-off populations (`FG/BB`) and the neural walk-off state consumed by the six-leg actuator.
