# FlyBrain V1.19.35 — Phasic High-Pass Locomotion Correction

## Why V1.19.34 was rejected

The V1.19.34 CI run did not pass its own Android unit tests. In particular, the six-leg
actuator reported insufficient physical support in symmetric phasic-drive tests. More
importantly, source review showed that the phasic decoder still used a slow baseline
(8–18 s) as its locomotor reference. A sustained high VNC rate could therefore remain
classified as "burst" for seconds after the neural rate stopped changing.

## V1.19.35 correction

The leg decoder now uses two temporal references:

- slow baseline: physiological/diagnostic reference;
- fast 220 ms baseline: phasic locomotor detector.

The locomotor target is generated only from rate excursions above the fast baseline plus
a measured rising component. Sustained constant motor firing is therefore absorbed by
the fast baseline instead of becoming an indefinitely renewable gait command.

This remains entirely downstream of the retained connectome:

`MaleCNS neural dynamics -> measured LEG-MN rates -> temporal phasic decoder -> six-leg actuator -> body`

No food, light, danger, action-selection variable or synthetic motor command enters the
actuator.

## Mechanical correction

`contact` now represents geometric stance support rather than neural load. Neural
activation continues to control `load` and propulsion. This separates:

- physical foot-ground contact;
- neural force generation.

That distinction is required for meaningful support/proprioceptive feedback and removes
the previous artificial rule in which a silent motor neuron made an already planted foot
cease to exist mechanically.

## Validation policy

The V1.19.34 failure is explicitly treated as a release blocker. V1.19.35 must pass:

- phasic high-pass static audit;
- release-version contract;
- six-leg JUnit suite;
- full Gradle debug unit tests;
- APK assembly.

No claim of APK validity is made until CI passes.
