# FlyBrain V1.19.20 — Final locomotion audit

## Scope

This document closes the **software locomotion stack** under the project's stated
limitations. It does not claim that the reduced 2-D model is a full biomechanical
reconstruction of adult *Drosophila melanogaster*.

The causal chain is:

`retained sensory input -> MaleCNS retained neural dynamics -> retained VNC leg motor output -> six-leg mechanics -> body physics`

No food/light/danger/action variable is allowed to write body position, heading or
leg activation directly.

## Neural layer

- Leg groups are populated from measured retained VNC motor-neuron spike output.
- Official MaleCNS motor semantics determine leg side/subclass and the six groups:
  LF, LM, LH, RF, RM, RH.
- A measured phasic motor excess drives the mechanical actuator; tonic baseline is
  treated as non-propulsive background.
- Walk-OFF is derived from retained neural halting populations.
- Proprioceptive/mechanosensory actuator state is returned to the retained sensory
  input layer; it is not used as a direct steering command.
- Olfactory input remains upstream of the connectome and is not read by the body
  actuator.

## Mechanical layer

- Six independent leg phases are retained.
- Stance/swing/contact/load/stride/lift are generated from each leg's measured motor
  activation.
- Forward propulsion is derived from backward foot motion during stance.
- Turning is derived from left/right propulsive imbalance and is gated by actual
  translation/propulsion.
- Lateral translation is gated by real propulsive drive; static support imbalance
  alone cannot move the body.
- Modified-tripod timing remains fixed as the reduced mechanical coordination model.
  This is an explicit model limitation: the reduced actuator does not claim to infer
  gait phase timing directly from individual MN spike timing.

## Physical layer

- Body velocity is integrated from actuator forward/lateral velocity.
- Heading is integrated from actuator yaw rate.
- World displacement is derived from the resulting body-frame velocity.
- Position remains bounded by the arena envelope.
- Wall collision removes inward velocity and applies tangential friction.
- Wall pressure is exposed to the mechanosensory pathway on the next neural update.
- Wall contact does not write yaw, leg phase or a synthetic escape direction.

## Explicitly prohibited shortcuts

The final actuator contains no direct use of:

- `foodOn`
- `foodX`
- `foodY`
- `lightOn`
- `dangerOn`
- `approachAction`
- `orientAction`
- random exploratory steering
- direct wall-to-yaw steering
- wall-induced gait phase kicks

## Dynamic acceptance tests

The isolated `LeggedSensorimotorActuator` runtime test passes:

1. Symmetric six-leg drive produces forward movement.
2. Symmetric drive produces near-zero mean yaw.
3. Right/left motor imbalance produces deterministic yaw with the established sign.
4. Walk-OFF brakes locomotion.
5. Removing motor output freezes leg phase and lets body velocity decay.
6. Wall contact produces mechanosensory pressure without synthetic yaw.
7. Wall contact does not modify gait phase.
8. Wall contact does not create artificial acceleration.

Observed symmetric-drive diagnostic from the final local dynamic run:

- mean yaw: approximately `-4.63e-4`
- temporally filtered bilateral propulsive symmetry: approximately `0.964`
- mean forward-force proxy: approximately `0.414`
- tripod phase coherence: `>0.99`

## What is closed

The **software causal locomotion architecture** is closed for V1.19.20: sensory
signals must pass through the retained neural substrate before becoming VNC motor
output, and the mechanical/body layer no longer contains an independent steering
controller or wall-escape controller.

## What remains outside the model's claim

The project still does not model full 3-D body dynamics, individual muscles and
motor units, tendon compliance, detailed joint geometry, experimentally calibrated
mass/inertia, or measured ground-reaction forces. Therefore this release should be
understood as a constrained neuromechanical approximation, not a claim of exact
biophysical equivalence to a real fly.
