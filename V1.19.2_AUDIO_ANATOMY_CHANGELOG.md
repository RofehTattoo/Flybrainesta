# V1.19.2 — Audio and anatomical presentation patch

## Scope
This patch changes only presentation/audio behavior in `MainActivity.kt` and the
`fly_buzz.wav` resource. It does not alter the connectome, FBR-10, neuron
selection, sensory routing, leg actuator, or locomotor physics.

## Audio
- Replaced the 1-second source tone with a 2-second, loop-oriented, harmonic-rich
  wingbeat-like buzz (48 kHz, mono PCM).
- Reduced the maximum playback volume.
- Sound activation now follows measured wing activity and body movement rather
  than stimulus presence alone.
- Playback rate varies slightly with activity to avoid a completely fixed pitch.

This is a designed approximation, not a recording of a real fly. Exact species,
sex, microphone distance, and recording conditions affect real wing sound.

## Visual anatomy
- Replaced the broad leaf-like wing silhouettes with narrower lanceolate wings,
  clearer costa/longitudinal veins, and fine cross-veins.
- Refined male abdominal coloration into amber-brown anterior tergites and a
  darker posterior/terminal region with subtler segment boundaries.
- Existing neural state and actuator outputs remain the source of visual motion.

## Validation and limitations
- Static source substitutions were checked while preparing the archive.
- Android compilation/device playback were not run in this environment.
- Review the audio on the target phone; perceived loudness and timbre depend on
  the device speaker. This is an improved synthesized effect, not a biological
  field recording.
