# FlyBrain V1.19.30 — Locomotion reconstruction phase 2

## Evidence basis

The V1.19.29 APK recording showed persistent wall/corner trapping despite the
six-leg refactor. Source inspection found that the mechanical abstraction still
treated tonic leg-MN firing as mostly non-propulsive and that wall interaction
remained a point-body collision: feet were animated but did not generate local
contact reaction mechanics.

## Changes

1. **Tonic + phasic neural leg drive**
   - Each of the six measured VNC LEG-MN groups contributes both instantaneous rate
     and phasic excess.
   - Baseline remains a normalization reference but no longer deletes sustained
     motor output.
   - No food/light/danger/action value is read by the mechanical actuator.

2. **Explicit six-foot force moment**
   - The actuator keeps six independent leg states.
   - Yaw uses the planar lever arm of each individual stance foot.
   - No direct left/right turn command is introduced.

3. **Physical wall/foot reaction**
   - When a stance foot's actual kinematic position intersects the arena envelope,
     a local reaction force is computed from measured stance load and geometric
     penetration.
   - The resulting `r x F` moment is applied as physical contact torque.
   - This is not an escape controller and does not prescribe a turn direction.
   - The retained mechanosensory pathway still receives wall contact separately.

## Explicit non-goals

- No modification to FBR-10 topology.
- No synthetic neurons or synapses.
- No target/action -> body shortcut.
- No random exploratory steering.
- No wallEscapeBias.
