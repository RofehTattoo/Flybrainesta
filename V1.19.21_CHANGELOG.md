# FlyBrain V1.19.21 — anatomical visual observatory correction

V1.19.21 is a presentation/validation correction on top of V1.19.20.

It does **not** expand FBR-10, add neurons, alter synaptic weights, or change
neural dynamics. The new layer makes the 2D neural observatory anatomically
meaningful and source-traceable.

## Main changes

1. A 16,669-row `anatomical_visual_map.tsv` is generated in CI from the official
   MaleCNS v1.0 annotations and the retained FBC103 body IDs.
2. Primary visual receptors (R1-6/R7/R8) are drawn at the compound-eye side;
   visual relays stay in the optic lobes.
3. Primary olfactory receptors are drawn at the antenna/maxillary sensory origin;
   olfactory central families may be represented in the antennal lobes.
4. Gustatory receptors are separated into tarsal peripheral sites and the SEZ
   (labellar/pharyngeal), matching the existing `gustSite` provenance layer.
5. Johnston's-organ mechanosensory receptors are mapped to the AMMC; leg and
   chordotonal populations are mapped to the VNC entry region.
6. VNC motor neurons are placed in the VNC and preserve the six LF/LM/LH/RF/RM/RH
   group assignments already derived from official motor semantics.
7. Ascending/descending populations are separated visually along the VNC-brain
   axis; conservative MB/CX/AL/SEZ family mapping is used for central neurons.
8. Every active retained neuron can be rendered even when it is outside the
   320-neuron representative graph sample.

## Scientific boundary

The map is a 2D anatomical schematic, not a 3D reconstruction of individual
soma/arbor coordinates. Where the available official annotation does not support
an exact neuropil assignment, the renderer deliberately uses `CENTRAL_BRAIN`
instead of inventing a precise coordinate.

Relevant anatomical references include the Drosophila adult-brain overview,
FlyWire annotation framework, and SEZ sensory anatomy literature.
