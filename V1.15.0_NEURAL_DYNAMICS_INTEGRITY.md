# FlyBrain V1.15.0 — Neural Dynamics Integrity

## Objective
T5A–T5D showed that V1.14.4 could register stimulus-dependent global activity while the aggregate DN/MN readouts remained highly active at baseline. V1.15.0 addresses the numerical/dynamical causes without changing the frozen FBR-10 structural graph.

## Structural layer — frozen
- MaleCNS v1.0 / FBR-10 / FBC103 remains unchanged.
- 16,669 neurons and 2,064,951 published edges remain the structural source of truth.
- The FBC103 SHA-256 remains `bfadc30fd113c25f9711cce6ef8f6b80e9c139fe6d229965a4adabb94d8b4e60`.

## Dynamics layer — FBD104
- Derived from the frozen FBC103 body IDs plus the pinned MaleCNS v1.0 neurotransmitter table.
- Acetylcholine = +1 fast sign.
- GABA = -1 fast sign.
- Glutamate = -1 under the project's established reduced-model convention.
- Other/modulatory/unknown transmitters contribute no fast synaptic current.
- Raw contact counts are normalized per postsynaptic target over recognized fast-synaptic contacts and scaled by 0.42, preserving the historical FlyBrain signed-LIF scaling convention.
- Unknown/modulatory edges are retained in FBC103 but omitted only from the fast-current dynamics layer.

## Runtime corrections
1. The adaptive synaptic-gain controller is removed from the experimental loop. Population gains are fixed model parameters during measurement.
2. `sensoryCurrent` is cleared at the start of every `sense()` call; sensory input is therefore a per-step external current rather than an accidental persistent state.
3. The runtime fails closed if FBD104 is missing, malformed, or inconsistent with FBC103.
4. Diagnostic values remain read-only and do not control neural or body dynamics.

## Scientific rationale
The purpose is not to make the fly perform a desired behavior. The purpose is to restore a signed, numerically controlled neural substrate so that behavior can emerge from the retained MaleCNS connectivity and measured motor output.
